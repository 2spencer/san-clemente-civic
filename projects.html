Capital Improvement Program (CIP)

FYs 2026/2027 and 2027/2028 CIP includes ~80 projects (capital and maintenance). Below are representative major projects drawn from the adopted CIP package and related agenda materials. Costs and timelines are as published; many are multi-year.

Beaches/ParksCapital

Army Corps Sand Replenishment

Federal/state/local coastal storm damage reduction project. Places sand on the beach near the pier on a multi-year cycle. PED phase in 2026/27; construction later subject to funding.

Est. total ~$25.5M multi-year · Target: Spring 2028 (PED)

Beaches/ParksCapital

Muni Golf Course Irrigation Replacement

Multi-phase replacement of the aging irrigation system to improve water efficiency and reliability.

Est. total ~$6.25M · Completion Spring 2028

StreetsCapital

Avenida Victoria Sidewalk Widening (Pier Underpass)

Widen sidewalk at the pier trolley stop / underpass to improve accessibility and reduce pedestrian-vehicle conflicts. Partial funding from PierPride Foundation.

Est. total ~$850K · Completion Spring 2028

StreetsCapital

Arterial Street Improvement Program

Citywide arterial rehabilitation (reconstruction of deteriorated areas, crack fill, overlay/slurry/micro).

Est. ~$750K per year · Gas Tax funded

StreetsCapital

Calafia Complete Streets

Bike lane/path and sidewalk improvements along Avenida Calafia from Del Presidente to the State Parks lot.

Est. total ~$1.15M · Completion Spring 2027

DrainageCapital

Poche Beach Water Reclamation Project

Replace UV system with dry-weather diversion to outfall for bacteria control and sea-level-rise resilience.

Est. total ~$5.1M · Completion Fall 2029

SewerCapital

Headworks Grit Cyclone Replacement

Replace aging grit removal equipment at the wastewater treatment plant headworks.

Est. total ~$1.1M · Completion Spring 2028

FacilitiesCapital

Steed Park Parking Lot Expansion

Add ~168 parking spaces plus circulation, ADA, drainage, and landscaping improvements.

Est. total ~$1.05M · Completion Spring 2028

Beaches/ParksCapital

Tierra Grande Park Playground Replacement

Remove and replace playground equipment with safety surfacing and ADA enhancements.

Est. total ~$325K · Completion Summer 2027

StreetsCapital

North Beach Train Station Upgrades

New pedestrian crosswalk with warning devices at Avenida Pico & Calle Deshecha, plus sidewalk bulb-outs and ADA ramps. SCAG FTIP grant support.

Est. total ~$960K · Completion Spring 2028
