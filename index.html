Property Tax
Largest single revenue source. Includes secured and unsecured property taxes. Distributions typically arrive in December and April. Recent years have shown steady growth tied to local development and assessed values.
Sales Tax
Primarily local Bradley-Burns and Measure-related sales tax. Sensitive to tourism, retail, and outlet activity. Recent reports show modest fluctuations year-over-year.
Other Taxes / Transient Occupancy Tax
Includes Transient Occupancy Tax (hotel tax), business license tax, and other minor tax sources. TOT is driven by lodging occupancy and rates.
Service Charges
Fees for services such as recreation programs, building permits, public works services, and other user fees. More variable than tax revenue.
Interfund Transfers & Other
Includes transfers from other funds (overhead charges, reimbursements) plus interest, rents, and miscellaneous revenue. These can swing based on capital project timing and investment performance.

Contractual Services — $54.1M
Largest category. Includes contracted public safety (Police ~$23.9M and Fire ~$15.3M are largely contract-based), professional services, maintenance contracts, and other outsourced work.
Salaries + Benefits — $24.0M
Direct city staff: Salaries ~$16.4M + Benefits ~$7.5M. Covers remaining in-house positions across departments (administration, community development, public works, recreation, marine safety, etc.).
Capital Outlay — $7.5M
Project and equipment spending. Ties heavily to the Capital Improvement Program (80 projects planned for FY 2026/27–2027/28 across beaches/parks, streets, drainage, facilities, and utilities).
Interdepartmental / Transfers & Debt — ~$10.3M
Internal allocations between funds plus debt service and transfers out (including to enterprise funds and capital reserves).
Supplies & Other — ~$3.2M
Materials, utilities, and miscellaneous operating costs.
