Largest single revenue source. Includes secured and unsecured. Distributions typically December and April. Steady growth tied to assessed values.
Sales Tax
Bradley-Burns and local measures. Sensitive to tourism, retail, and outlet activity.
Other Taxes / TOT
Transient Occupancy Tax (hotel), business license, and minor taxes. Driven by lodging occupancy.
Service Charges
Recreation programs, permits, public works fees, and other user fees.
Interfund & Other
Transfers, interest, rents, and miscellaneous. Can swing with capital timing and investments.
